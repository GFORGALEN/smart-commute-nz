Privacy

Updated 9 October 2026.

Manual calculations run in your browser. User-entered settings, places and routes are saved in this browser's localStorage. You can remove them with the reset control or your browser's site-data controls. We do not operate an account database, analytics or an AI service.

The production service worker saves this website's own files for offline use. It does not save Google Maps/Places/Routes or Auckland Transport responses. GitHub Pages hosts this public website; hosting requests are subject to GitHub's privacy statement.

Your device's location is requested only when you click the location button and allow browser permission. It is used as a user-supplied origin. No background location tracking occurs.

If an operator configures a Google browser key and you explicitly activate Google services, your location queries and network information are sent to Google. Google content is kept in the active planning session; user-authored inputs are the only route data we persist. See Google's Privacy Policy and Google Maps terms. Google may use its own storage and cookies according to its policies.

Do not publish a private home address in an issue. For questions or defects, use the project issue tracker.
